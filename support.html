Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Use the Forgot Password option on the login screen.

How do I recover access if my phone is lost?

Log in from another device using your registered email and recovery options.

How do I remove a registered device?

Open Device Settings and select Remove Device.

How do I report a bug?

Send a detailed description, screenshots, and device information to our support email.
